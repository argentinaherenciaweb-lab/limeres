## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-end, discreet, and authoritative aesthetic tailored for cross-border inheritance, probate, and estate administration in Argentina for U.S. citizens, expatriates, and international heirs. 

The visual style blends **Modern Minimalism** with **Editorial Legal Heritage**. Moving away from generic template aesthetics, it balances deep charcoal monolithic headers, crisp off-white canvas spaces, warm architectural photography, and quiet metallic gold and slate accents. The emotional impression communicates fiduciary gravity, effortless competence, international trust, and legal precision.

Key attributes:
- **Discreet Luxury:** Clean structural alignments, generous margins, restrained accent colors, and tactile micro-details.
- **Fiduciary Authority:** High-contrast type hierarchy using classical editorial serifs anchored by crisp, technical sans-serifs.
- **Action-Oriented Clarity:** Low-friction consultation pathways, persistent context, and focused form architecture designed to eliminate legal intimidation.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep charcoal foundations paired with pure canvas whites and tailored accents.

- **Primary (`#121417`):** Deep obsidian charcoal used for dominant navigation rails, key legal section blocks, high-contrast headings, and grounding buttons.
- **Secondary (`#1E3A8A`):** Deep Diplomatic Slate Blue reflecting international treaty authority, transatlantic communications, and secondary callouts without the aggressive vibrance of standard web blue.
- **Tertiary (`#BFA15F`):** Subtle heirloom gold representing generational wealth, probate security, and discreet legal seal accents.
- **Neutral (`#717680`):** Balanced granite gray used for explanatory subheadings, formal meta text, and document captions.

### Accent & Background Pairings
Dark sections employ `canvas-dark` (`#0B0C0E`) and `surface-dark` (`#16181D`) with subtle hairline dividers (`#272A30`). Light editorial content rests on `canvas-light` (`#FBFBFA`) and pure white cards with soft neutral borders (`#E5E7EB`). Never use bright saturated gradients; gold is used strictly as a structural stroke, highlight badge, or interactive hover accent.

## Typography

The typography unites traditional legal authority with effortless modern scanning:

- **Headlines (`Newsreader`):** An elegant, scholarly serif with crisp proportions that brings stately editorial prestige to international law topics. Used with italicized highlights for emphasis on jurisdiction, estate assets, or client guarantees.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** A geometric, approachable sans-serif offering immaculate legibility across legal intake questions, long-form statutory explanations, and desktop-to-mobile consultation forms.
- **Eyebrows & Metadata:** Set in small, tracked-out uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`letterSpacing: 0.12em`) to categorize practice areas (e.g., `ESTATE PLANNING`, `FARMLAND SUCCESSION`, `APOSTILLE & POWER OF ATTORNEY`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional 12-column architectural grid with generous breathing room reflecting boutique legal exclusivity.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width container of `1280px` centered with `margin: 4rem` padding. Dual-column layouts dominate lead generation pages (5 columns for high-intent intake form, 7 columns for credentials, client testimonials, and case summaries).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `gutter: 1.5rem` and `margin: 2.5rem`. Service matrices adapt from 3 columns to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked flow with `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Sticky bottom consultation bar snaps to the bottom viewport.
- **Rhythm:** Sections breathe with alternating densities: legal cards and forms maintain a tight `space-lg` (1.5rem) internal rhythm, framed by generous section breaks (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed through **subtle surface contrast, architectural hairlines, and soft diffused shadows**. Heavy drop shadows are strictly avoided to retain institutional credibility.

- **Level 0 (Flat/Canvas):** Default page baseline (`canvas-light` or `canvas-dark`) without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card/Container):** Pure white or dark card surfaces with a 1px border (`border-subtle`: `#E5E7EB` on light; `#272A30` on dark) and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 6px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Intake Forms):** Interactive service cards shift up by 2px on hover with elevation: `0 12px 32px rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.04)`. The intake form maintains Level 2 elevation to anchor user focus.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bar & Overlays):** Fixed bottom consultation bars and modals leverage `rgba(18, 20, 23, 0.92)` backdrop blur (`12px`) with border top `#272A30` or light modal shadow `0 24px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural **Soft (Level 1)** geometry (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius), reinforcing legal precision, structure, and formal confidence.

- **Buttons, Form Fields & Cards:** Uniform `4px` corner radius. Sharp enough to feel legally binding and corporate, yet softened enough to prevent visual harshness.
- **Badges & Eyebrow Chips:** `2px` to `4px` radius; pill shapes are avoided to preserve a dignified, non-casual posture.
- **Framed Images & Testimonial Containers:** Fixed `4px` radius with a 1px inner or outer hairline border to mimic fine legal parchment framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (Consultation & Action):** Deep charcoal (`#121417`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `label-lg` uppercase tracking, `height: 48px`, padding `0 1.75rem`, `4px` border radius. On hover, transitions to subtle charcoal blue `#1A202C` with a gold accent underline or gold micro-border (`#BFA15F`).
- **Secondary Button (Phone / WhatsApp):** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#121417` (light mode) or `#FFFFFF` (dark mode). Direct text color match.
- **Tertiary Accent CTA:** Slate blue (`#1E3A8A`) background for cross-border phone bookings with a discrete telephone or calendar icon.

### Form Elements & Lead Generation Intake
- **Form Card:** Floated card on `surface-white` with a `1px solid #E5E7EB` border, framed with an elegant gold top stroke (`3px solid #BFA15F`).
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** Height `48px`, `border: 1px solid #D1D5DB`, background `#FFFFFF`, font `body-md`, `4px` radius. Active/Focus state uses `#121417` border with a subtle 2px ring in `#EEF2FF`. Floating labels or crisp micro-eyebrow labels above fields ensure maximum clarity for cross-border heirs.
- **Intake Trust Badges:** Placed directly beneath the submit trigger: displays U.S.-Argentina bilingual hotline, confidentiality guarantee ("Attorney-Client Privilege Preserved"), and 24-hour response SLA.

### Service & Practice Area Cards
- Minimalist rectangular panels with `padding: 2rem`, crisp typography hierarchy (`headline-sm` serif/sans mix), line-art legal/real-estate icons tinted in deep slate blue or gold, and a bottom "View Practice Guidelines →" text link that transitions color on hover.

### Sticky Consultation Element
- **Desktop/Mobile Sticky Bar:** Anchors to the viewport base with a high-contrast dark charcoal background (`#0B0C0E`), subtle top hairline (`#272A30`), showing attorney direct availability ("Argentina & U.S. Jurisdictions | Next Available Consultation Today") paired with a direct "Schedule Case Review" button.

### Logo Presentation
- Reuses the official black-and-white LIMERES wordmark. On dark headers, the white mark sits cleanly with a generous `space-lg` clear space; on light surfaces, it renders in pure jet black with no embellishments.
